Support

Questions or problems with Velora? Email anto.developer@hotmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you're signed in, so it follows you to a new device.

I bought a plan — how do I get it on a new phone? Use the same Apple ID; your plan is restored automatically. You can also use Restore on the plans screen.

How do I cancel? Settings → Cancel or change in App Store.
