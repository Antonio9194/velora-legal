Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Velora ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what Velora stores and where.

Data Velora stores

  • Expenses, subscriptions, budgets and piggy jars you enter, manually or with the assistant
  • Your language and currency preferences

There is no account and no email address. Everything is stored on your device and, if you are signed into iCloud, synced to your own private iCloud storage so it follows you to your other devices. We have no server and cannot see your expenses or budgets.

Assistant and insights

Quick add (text, voice or receipt photos) and spending insights are processed on your device using Apple's built-in models and Vision. Photos and voice messages are not stored or sent to us or to any AI company.

Advertising measurement

Velora includes Meta's SDK so we can tell whether our ads lead to installs and subscriptions. It sends app events such as installs, app launches and purchases, with a device identifier, to Meta. Your expenses, budgets and other financial details are never sent. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not collected. See Meta's privacy policy for how Meta handles this data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No device token is collected.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Velora reads your active subscription from the App Store on your device. We never see your payment details.

How we use your data

  • We do not sell your data.
  • Your financial data is never used for advertising.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase all data removes your expenses, subscriptions, budgets and jars from your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy.
